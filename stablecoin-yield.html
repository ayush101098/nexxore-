Strategy Types

🏦 Lending Yield

Deposit stablecoins on Aave, Compound, or Morpho. Earn variable interest from borrowers. Low risk, 2–6% APY.

💧 Liquidity Provision

Provide stablecoins to Curve or Uniswap pools. Earn trading fees + incentive tokens. Medium risk, 4–15% APY.

🏛️ RWA Vaults

Deposit into T-bill-backed vaults via Ondo, Mountain, or Maker DSR. Institutional-grade, 4–5% APY.

🔄 Yield Aggregators

Auto-compounding via Yearn, Beefy, or Sommelier. Strategies rotate to maximize risk-adjusted returns.

⚡ Leveraged Yield

Loop stablecoins across lending protocols for amplified returns. Higher risk, requires active monitoring.

🛡️ Delta-Neutral

Pair stablecoin lending with short perp positions. Capture funding rates while staying market-neutral.

Risk-Adjusted Scoring

Nexxore's stablecoin yield scoring weights multiple risk factors to produce a single risk-adjusted APY figure:

  • Smart Contract Risk (25%) — audit history, exploit incidents, code complexity
  • De-Peg Risk (20%) — peg deviation history, backing transparency, redemption mechanism
  • Protocol Maturity (15%) — time in production, TVL history, governance track record
  • Counterparty Risk (15%) — issuer jurisdiction, regulatory exposure, custodian quality
  • Yield Sustainability (15%) — source of yield (real vs inflationary), historical consistency
  • Liquidity Risk (10%) — withdrawal availability, pool depth, redemption delays

Frequently Asked Questions

What are stablecoin yield strategies?

Stablecoin yield strategies generate returns on dollar-pegged assets like USDC, USDT, and DAI. Common approaches include: lending on Aave or Compound (2–6% APY), providing liquidity in stablecoin pools on Curve (3–12% APY), and depositing into RWA-backed vaults that invest in T-bills (4–5% APY).

Are stablecoin yields safe?

Stablecoin yields carry several risk layers: smart contract risk (protocol exploits), de-peg risk (stablecoin losing its $1 peg), counterparty risk (centralized stablecoins depend on issuers), and yield compression (APYs can drop as more capital enters). Nexxore's risk-adjusted scoring helps compare these risks across strategies.

What stablecoins does Nexxore track?

Nexxore tracks yields for USDC, USDT, DAI, FRAX, crvUSD, GHO (Aave), LUSD (Liquity), sDAI, and USDe (Ethena). Each stablecoin includes backing composition data, de-peg history, and available yield opportunities.

What is risk-adjusted yield?

Risk-adjusted yield accounts for the probability and magnitude of potential losses. A 10% APY with high smart contract risk may have a lower risk-adjusted yield than a 4% APY in an audited, battle-tested protocol. Nexxore calculates risk-adjusted scores using audit status, TVL history, de-peg incidents, and protocol maturity.

How do RWA-backed stablecoin yields work?

Real World Asset (RWA) stablecoin yields come from traditional finance instruments — typically US Treasury bills (T-bills). Protocols like Ondo, Mountain, and Maker's DSR invest stablecoin deposits into T-bills and pass the yield to depositors. Current rates are 4–5% APY, backed by low-risk government securities.
