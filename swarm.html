Calibration

The log the swarm answers to.

Every run files a probability call. One hour later it resolves against the actual price and is Brier-scored — 0.0 is prophecy, 0.25 is a coin flip. Per-source hits and misses feed straight back into the fusion weights. Real money stays gated behind a written Brier improvement, not vibes.
