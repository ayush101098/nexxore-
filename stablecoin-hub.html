How this works

Risk score

Every pool gets a transparent Low / Medium / High band — no black-box grade. Points are added for:

  • Low TVL — under $50M (+1), under $10M (+2). Thin pools are easier to drain or exit badly.
  • High APY — over 12% (+1), over 20% (+2). Outsized yield usually means outsized risk.
  • Impermanent-loss exposure (+2) and multi-asset pools (+1), from DeFiLlama's classification.
  • APY volatility (+1) when the pool's historical yield swings widely.

0–1 → Low · 2–3 → Medium · 4+ → High. It's a heuristic to sort candidates, not a guarantee.

Data & strategies

All yields, TVL, and pool classifications come from DeFiLlama's public API, refreshed on this page every 2 minutes; peg prices come from DeFiLlama's stablecoin feed. Everything is the same canonical dataset — the stat strip, table, and strategies never disagree.

Strategies are assembled live: we take the top pools within each risk profile, weight them, and show the blended APY. "View" links open the pool on DeFiLlama so you can verify and act at the source. Nexxore does not custody funds or execute these trades.

Not financial advice. Stablecoin yields carry smart-contract, de-peg, counterparty, and liquidity risk, and advertised APY can change or not materialize. Data may be delayed or incorrect. Do your own research, verify every protocol and contract independently, and never deposit more than you can afford to lose.
