Frequently Asked Questions

What metrics does Nexxore track for strategy performance?

Nexxore tracks P&L (realized and unrealized), Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, maximum drawdown, Calmar ratio, win rate, average win/loss, profit factor, and time-weighted returns. All metrics update in real-time for active strategies.

Can I backtest DeFi strategies on Nexxore?

Yes. Nexxore provides historical data for backtesting perpetual, options, and yield strategies. Specify entry/exit rules, position sizing, and risk parameters to evaluate how your strategy would have performed over historical data.

What types of strategies can I track?

Nexxore supports tracking for: perpetual futures (directional and basis trades), options strategies (straddles, strangles, spreads), yield farming (single-sided and LP), delta-neutral positions, and hybrid strategies combining multiple instruments.

How is Sharpe ratio calculated?

The Sharpe ratio is calculated as (strategy return − risk-free rate) / standard deviation of returns. In crypto, we typically use stablecoin lending rates (3–5% APY) as the risk-free rate. A Sharpe ratio above 1.0 is considered good; above 2.0 is excellent.

Does Nexxore support multi-chain strategy tracking?

Yes. Track strategies across Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Solana, and Polygon. Cross-chain strategies are aggregated into a single P&L view with chain-specific breakdowns available.
