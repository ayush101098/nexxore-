Why Vault Risk Analysis Matters

In DeFi, chasing yield without understanding risk is a path to permanent capital loss. Nexxore's vault risk analysis provides the due diligence framework used by institutional allocators:

  • Smart contract exploits have caused over $3B in losses since 2020
  • Unsustainable APYs driven by token emissions collapse when incentives end
  • Oracle manipulation and governance attacks remain persistent vectors
  • Chain-specific risks (bridge dependencies, sequencer downtime) often go unassessed

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DeFi vault risk analysis?

DeFi vault risk analysis evaluates the safety and sustainability of yield-generating strategies. It covers smart contract risk, protocol governance risk, impermanent loss exposure, APY sustainability, and historical drawdown analysis to help investors make informed allocation decisions.

How does Nexxore score vault risk?

Nexxore uses a multi-factor risk scoring model covering: (1) Smart contract audit status, (2) Protocol TVL and stability, (3) Historical APY variance, (4) Maximum drawdown, (5) Governance centralization, (6) Oracle dependency, and (7) Chain-specific risks. Scores range from A (lowest risk) to D (highest risk).

Which DeFi protocols does Nexxore analyze?

Nexxore covers 50+ DeFi protocols including Aave, Compound, Lido, MakerDAO, Curve, Convex, Yearn, Pendle, Morpho, Ethena, and more across Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, and Solana.

What is APY sustainability analysis?

APY sustainability analysis evaluates whether a vault's current yield is driven by organic revenue (sustainable) or by temporary incentives like token emissions (unsustainable). Nexxore decomposes yield into base rate, incentive rate, and bonus components.

How do I compare vault strategies?

Use Nexxore's vault comparison tool to evaluate strategies side-by-side across risk score, historical APY, max drawdown, TVL trend, and chain exposure. Filter by risk tolerance (conservative, moderate, aggressive) to find strategies matching your profile.
