Frequently Asked Questions

What are on-chain derivatives?

On-chain derivatives are financial contracts (futures, options, swaps) that settle on a blockchain. Unlike centralized exchange derivatives, on-chain derivatives offer transparency, self-custody, and composability with other DeFi protocols. Major platforms include dYdX, GMX, Hyperliquid, and Aevo.

What derivatives data does Nexxore track?

Nexxore tracks: open interest (by asset, exchange, and chain), trading volume, funding rates, implied volatility surfaces, options greeks, liquidation events, basis spreads, and term structure data across both DeFi and CeFi venues.

How is implied volatility useful for crypto trading?

Implied volatility (IV) reflects the market's expectation of future price movement. High IV means options are expensive (market expects big moves). IV can be used for: timing entries (buy when IV is low), selling premium (sell options when IV is high), and gauging market sentiment.

What is a liquidation cascade?

A liquidation cascade occurs when forced liquidations at one price level trigger further price movement, leading to more liquidations in a chain reaction. Nexxore tracks liquidation clusters and alerts traders to price levels where cascades are likely.

Can I compare DeFi and CeFi derivatives markets?

Yes. Nexxore provides side-by-side comparison of derivatives data across DeFi (dYdX, GMX, Hyperliquid) and CeFi (Binance, Bybit, OKX). Compare open interest, volume, funding rates, and spread quality across both market types.
