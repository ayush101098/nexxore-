Method & honesty note. Backtest figures ($200→$25,400, 74% win, Sharpe 2.47) are a strategy target, not a live audited account. Everything tagged Live is computed in-browser from Polymarket's public Gamma, Data and CLOB APIs. The three agents are transparent quantitative models — Smart-Money (whale/elite flow), Flow (momentum + volume), Value (order-book imbalance + liquidity) — not black boxes. Win rate is measured from each wallet's positions currently in profit and is a proxy, not a settled-trade audit. Nothing here is financial advice.
