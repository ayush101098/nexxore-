# NEXXORE glossary — canonical terms

One vocabulary across product, marketing, and code. Use these exactly; do not
introduce synonyms.

| Term | Definition | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| **Regime** | The market's structural state, derived from the Escalation Index: `risk-on`, `transition`, or `risk-off`. | "market mode", "phase", "condition", "environment" |
| **Escalation Index** | The 0–1 composite signal (conflict, sanctions, energy, macro) that determines the regime. | "risk score", "fear index" |
| **The loop** | The Sense → Decide → Act cycle the whole product runs on. | "the pipeline", "the flow" |
| **Sense / Decide / Act** | The three loop stages. Products map to exactly one stage. | — |
| **Proof Layer** | The on-chain, verifiable record of performance. Until it ships, no performance figures are shown. | "track record" (as a live claim) |
| **Reported vs. Verified** | A "reported" number is asserted with a source note; a "verified" number is computed from the Proof Layer. | "confirmed", "audited" (unless via Proof Layer) |

## Voice

- Sentence case for headings. No exclamation marks.
- Numerals for numbers (`6 chains`, not "six chains").
- Status is only ever one of: **LIVE · SIMULATED · IN AUDIT · PENDING** (the `LiveBadge` variants).
- Regime colors are informational only. Cyan is the only interactive color.
