What Is the {{TOKEN_SYMBOL}} Funding Rate?

The funding rate is a periodic payment exchanged between long and short traders on {{TOKEN_SYMBOL}} perpetual futures. It keeps the perpetual contract price anchored to the {{TOKEN_SYMBOL}} spot price.

  • Positive rate → longs pay shorts → market is net-long (bullish bias)
  • Negative rate → shorts pay longs → market is net-short (bearish bias)
  • Near-zero rate → balanced positioning → no directional edge from funding

Why Funding Rates Matter for {{TOKEN_SYMBOL}} Traders

Funding rates reveal the prevailing market sentiment and create two distinct trading opportunities:

  • Carry trade — collect funding by taking the less-crowded side while hedging with spot
  • Sentiment signal — extreme positive funding often precedes corrections; extreme negative funding often precedes squeezes
