Yield by Strategy Type

🏦 Lending (Lowest Risk)

Deposit stablecoins into lending protocols. Earn interest from borrowers. Typical range: 2–8% APY. Protocols: Aave, Compound, Morpho, Spark.

💧 Liquidity Provision (Medium Risk)

Provide stablecoins to AMM pools. Earn trading fees + incentive tokens. Typical range: 4–15% APY. Protocols: Curve, Uniswap, Aerodrome, Velodrome.

🏛️ RWA Vaults (Low Risk)

Deposit into T-bill-backed vaults. Earn treasury yield. Typical range: 4–5% APY. Protocols: Ondo, Mountain, Maker DSR.

⚡ Yield Tokenisation (Variable Risk)

Split yield-bearing stablecoins into principal and yield tokens via Pendle. Lock in fixed rates or speculate on yield direction. Range: 5–30% APY depending on market conditions.

🔄 Delta-Neutral Carry (Higher Risk)

Pair stablecoin collateral with short perps to capture funding rates. Typical range: 8–25% APY. Ethena's USDe is the largest automated version of this strategy.

What is the safest stablecoin yield strategy?

The safest stablecoin yield strategies are: lending USDC on Aave or Compound (2–5% APY, audited blue-chip protocols), Maker DAI Savings Rate via sDAI (currently {{SDAI_APY}}), and T-bill-backed vaults from Ondo or Mountain Protocol (4–5% APY, backed by US Treasuries).

Is USDe yield sustainable?

USDe (Ethena) generates yield from delta-neutral positions that capture perpetual funding rates. When funding is positive, yields can reach 15–25% APY. However, this is cyclical — during bear markets or negative funding, yields compress significantly.

How do RWA stablecoin yields work?

RWA stablecoin yields come from US Treasury bills. Protocols like Ondo (USDY), Mountain (USDM), and Maker (sDAI with RWA exposure) invest stablecoin deposits into T-bill vehicles and pass the yield to holders. Current rates are 4–5% APY.

What risks do stablecoin yields carry?

Key risks: smart contract exploits, de-peg events, counterparty risk, regulatory risk, and yield compression. Always assess the source of yield — organic (borrowing demand, fees) is more sustainable than inflationary (token emissions).
