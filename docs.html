Nexxore docs

Nexxore is a probability layer for prediction markets. For a market, it puts a probability next to the price, works out the edge after the spread and fees, shows how much size you can take before that edge disappears, and logs every call so it can be scored when the market resolves.

Everything here is research. Nexxore does not hold funds, and apart from the Strategy Builder's Deribit integration (which uses your own API key), it does not place orders.

Prediction Terminal

A live view of Polymarket: prices, 24h volume, liquidity, spread, resolution date, price history and the order book for the selected market.

  • Your estimate and net edge. Enter your probability for a market. Net edge is your estimate minus the price you would actually pay: the best ask for YES, or one minus the best bid for NO. The scanner can rank markets by it.
  • Size before the edge is gone. The order book is walked level by level to find how much you can buy before the average fill reaches your estimate.
  • Bracket consistency. In events with mutually exclusive outcomes, the YES prices should sum to about 100%. The terminal flags events where they don't, after the cost of crossing every spread.

Alerts

Watch markets, set a move threshold, preview what would fire, then enable. Alerts are delivered in the page and as browser notifications while the page is open. An alert never posts anywhere or places a trade.

Strategy Builder

Build options and perpetual positions on BTC, ETH and SOL from a thesis and a template, stress-test them against time and implied volatility, and review max loss, breakevens, capital, estimated fees, funding and liquidation before committing. Strategies can be saved in your browser.

The risk numbers are model estimates: lognormal price paths, 10× perp margin and Deribit taker fees. They are not backtests.

Risk Engine

Value at risk, expected shortfall and regime-adjusted Kelly sizing from 180 days of daily closes. The escalation triggers mix live and simulated inputs, and each one is labelled.

Probability & edge

A market price is the crowd's probability. Edge is the gap between a better probability and the price you can actually trade at, after:

  • Spread: you buy at the ask, not the midpoint.
  • Fees: where a market charges them.
  • Depth: larger orders fill at worse prices, so edge shrinks with size.

On thin markets the spread is often the whole edge, which is why every number on Nexxore is quoted after it.

The public ledger

The track record publishes every model forecast with a timestamp. For each call it records:

  1. The model's probability and the market price at the same moment
  2. The outcome when the market resolves
  3. A Brier score for the model and for the market price
  4. Edge after the spread and fees

The headline number is whether the model's Brier score beats the market's own. A lower Brier is better: 0 is perfect, and always answering 50% scores 0.25. No calls have resolved yet, so the ledger is empty, and it says so.

Data sources

  • Polymarket: markets, prices, order books, trades and the leaderboard, from Polymarket's public APIs.
  • Manifold: shown for comparison only. Manifold uses play money.
  • Binance and Deribit: spot, perp, funding and options data.
  • DefiLlama: stablecoin pools and pegs.
