The Risk Intelligence Stack

From regime detection to structured exposure. Three layers of risk infrastructure.

01

Regime Detection Engine

Agents ingest multi-source data to model probabilistic regime states — not price targets.

  • On-chain capital flows
  • Funding rate stress
  • Order book depth shifts
  • Sentiment dispersion
  • Volatility clustering
Output: Risk-adjusted regime probability. Transition risk is measured before exposure is deployed.
02

Capital Allocation Engine

Regime probabilities are transformed into structured exposures, evaluated through drawdown modeling and expected shortfall.

  • Delta-neutral carry
  • Hedged perpetual structures
  • Stablecoin defense frameworks
  • Volatility capture strategies
Evaluated through: Drawdown modeling, CVaR (Expected Shortfall), liquidity stress testing.
03

Execution & Liquidity Routing

Exposure is deployed across chains with capital efficiency as the primary constraint.

  • Slippage-aware routing
  • Liquidity fragmentation optimization
  • Cross-chain hedge coordination
Principle: Intent is abstracted. Execution is capital-efficient.
