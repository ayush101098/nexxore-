How it works
Three layers. One pipeline.

Data flows in one direction: prediction → strategy → execution. Each layer does one thing well and hands off to the next.

01 — PREDICT

The intelligence layer

Agents ingest on-chain flows, funding rates, social sentiment, and order book data. They look for the things that cause price to move — before the move happens.

Most platforms react to price. We're trying to predict volatility. Big difference.
→
02 — STRATEGIZE

The logic layer

Raw signals get turned into actual trade logic. "If funding flips negative and OI spikes, open a hedged position." You can build this visually or via the API.

Supports multi-leg trades, backtesting, and risk parameters. Pro traders design it, passive depositors use it.
→
03 — EXECUTE

The muscle layer

The engine finds the best liquidity across DEXs and chains, manages slippage, and settles the trade. No human in the loop. If the best fill is on Arbitrum but the signal came from Solana — it handles it.

Think of it as a routing engine, not a single exchange. It picks the best path for every trade.
