08 · The gate — five checks before paper
·Run a backtest to evaluate the gate.
Backtests replay real exchange history (last 1,000 bars) with taker fees + slippage modeled at 14bps per round trip and intrabar stop/target fills. The final 30% of bars are held out-of-sample. A backtest is a promise — paper trading is the receipt.
